For the love of making

I've always loved tinkering with my hands. This page collects what I've posted as @aqsamakesthings that isn't already on the Work page: teaching models, Makerspace workshops, gifts for friends, signs for student groups, and art.

01 · Teaching with my hands

Making abstract biology tactile

As a Chancellor's Undergraduate Teaching Fellow for BIOENG 1070 Cell Biology (2025–2026), I was selected for a competitive fellowship to support a redesign of the course around hands-on, active learning. With faculty mentors Dr. Carsten Stuckenholz and Dr. Lance Davidson, I developed biweekly tactile activities that reinforce abstract biological concepts, along with supplemental digital resources and summative review videos. These are some of the experiential learning modules.

02 · Makerspace workshops

Workshops and shared making

As a mentor and program manager at the Pitt Makerspace, I designed and led workshops to get more students from every discipline using the tools. I also ran moulage and silicone wound-effects workshops, including one for Pitt Student EMS; those photos aren't shown here.

03 · Built for fun (and for friends)

Gifts, gadgets, signs, and art

Things I've made for friends, for the student groups I belong to, and for myself, using laser cutting, CNC routing, soldering, sewing, 3D printing, CAD, and paint. Studying Renaissance art in Florence also reignited my love for drawing and painting.

Otherwise, you'll find me

Painting in watercolor, reading coming-of-age novels, running, baking, riding roller coasters, and getting up early for a sunrise.
